What the tool does

  • Uploads and schedules videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, translations and playlists on the owner's channels.
  • Reads the owner's own channel analytics to decide which topics and formats work best.
  • Posts the channel's own discussion question and replies to viewer comments on the owner's adult channels (never on made-for-kids videos).
