Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers Safe Living Agent (the "App"), a private tool operated by Shivam Shiv Giri ("we") to publish videos to our own YouTube channels, Safe Living USA and Mini Mind World. The App uses YouTube API Services.

Who uses the App

Only the channel owner signs in to the App. It is not offered to the public, and we do not ask other people to connect their Google accounts.

What data the App accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the App accesses the following on the owner's own channels:

  • Uploading and managing videos, thumbnails, playlists and video translations (YouTube Data API).
  • Reading channel and video information, and public comments left by viewers on our videos (YouTube Data API).
  • Reading aggregate channel analytics such as views and watch time (YouTube Analytics API). These reports contain no personal information about individual viewers.

How we use it

  • To upload and schedule our videos and keep their details up to date.
  • To learn from aggregate analytics which topics and formats viewers find useful.
  • To reply to viewer comments on Safe Living USA. Comment text is used only to write a reply and is not stored. Only comment IDs are kept, so the App never replies twice to the same comment.

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens and analytics summaries are stored only on the owner's private computer, in files readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on a public server. Analytics summaries are kept for as long as they are useful for planning videos and can be deleted at any time.

Text generation

Scripts and comment replies are drafted with an AI language model. When replying to a comment, the public comment text is sent to that model to write the reply.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

YouTube and Google

By using features of the App that rely on YouTube, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the App can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

Children

Mini Mind World is a made-for-kids channel. The App does not collect any information from children. Comments are disabled on made-for-kids videos by YouTube, and the App does not read or reply to them.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: shivucuiseai@gmail.com.
